Menzies Aviation Bengaluru

01 — The Challenge

An airside facility on a live airport.

Menzies Aviation handles ground operations for multiple airlines at Kempegowda International Airport. Their new ground-handling facility had to be built airside-adjacent, with all the security clearances, restricted-access work windows, and zero-disruption requirements that come with an operating airport. The brief covered civil, structural, architectural, PEB, flooring, PHE, electrical, fire, HVAC, interior, façade, road works, compound walls, ancillary buildings, landscape and external development — a true single-source turnkey scope.

02 — Our Approach

Single-GC coordination across 14 disciplines.

SPC was the sole accountable contractor. Every airside permit, every restricted-hours pour, every security escort was coordinated through one project management chain. The schedule was sequenced backward from Menzies's go-live date for new airline contracts — non-negotiable.

  • Civil + PEB: RCC substructure with PEB superstructure for clear-span operational areas
  • Façade: Architectural cladding that meets airport visibility regulations
  • MEPF: Full electrical, fire, HVAC and plumbing in-house coordinated
  • Wraparound: Roads, compound walls, ancillary, landscape — handed over together
