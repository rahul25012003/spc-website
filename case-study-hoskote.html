Hoskote Logistics

5.8 lakh sft of logistics facility built at Hoskote — civil, structural, architectural, flooring, road works, electrical, PHE, fire tank and external works.

01 — The Challenge

Half a million sft. Eight months.

Hoskote Logistics needed warehouse space — fast. The brief was a half-million-plus square feet of usable logistics floor area, with full road infrastructure, electrical, plumbing, fire tank, and external works ready for tenant fit-out within eight months of contract signing. On a project of this footprint, every week of delay translates to weeks of lost rental income on already-signed tenant contracts.

02 — Our Approach

Parallel pour cycles + early flooring.

The schedule was built around overlapping pour cycles — multiple sections of the facility under construction simultaneously, each at a different stage. This is the only way to compress a 5.8L sft project into 8 months. Industrial-grade flooring (a critical path item for logistics tenants) was started as soon as RCC sections cured, not held until the end.

  • Civil + structural: Multi-bay RCC system with overlapping pour cycles
  • Flooring: Industrial-grade epoxy floors, started in sections as RCC cured
  • External: Internal roads, electrical, PHE and fire tank in parallel with main build

04 — Result

Delivered on time. Tenants moved in week one.

The full 5,81,627 sft was handed over within the 8-month commitment. Tenant fit-outs began immediately. This delivery model — overlap pours plus rolling flooring — has become our template for any logistics project above 5 lakh sft.
