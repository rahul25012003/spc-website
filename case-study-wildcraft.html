01 — The Challenge

An industrial campus, 2 hours from Bengaluru.

Wildcraft India needed a 2.89 lakh sft manufacturing and warehousing facility at Sira in Tumakuru district. Off-site logistics, labour camp arrangements, material movement from Bengaluru — all the challenges of a remote build, plus a full scope: civil, structural, PEB, façade, interior, electrical, plumbing, STP and fire fighting.

02 — Our Approach

PEB-led delivery with parallel MEPF.

The structural shell ran on PEB for the warehouse and large-span manufacturing areas. RCC was used selectively for the office block. This saved ~6 weeks against an all-RCC alternative. Façade, MEPF and fire fighting ran in parallel with the PEB erection.

  • PEB superstructure: 25m clear-span bays for manufacturing flexibility
  • Façade: Insulated panel system with branded cladding
  • STP + Fire fighting: Integrated from day one of MEPF rough-in
  • Logistics: Dedicated stores manager + scheduled material runs from Bengaluru

03 — Result

Delivered on time. Production-ready week one.

The facility was handed over to Wildcraft within the agreed 10-month window, including full STP and fire fighting commissioning. Wildcraft began production-line installation immediately.
