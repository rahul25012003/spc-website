Rossell Techsys

A 2,40,000 sft aerospace facility at KIADB Devanahalli — civil, high-side plumbing, interiors and external development.

01 — The Challenge

An aerospace facility with high-side plumbing.

Rossell Techsys manufactures aerospace components — work that demands specialised facility design, including high-pressure plumbing for cleaning systems, clean-room-adjacent interior finishes, and access control. The build was at KIADB Devanahalli, in the aerospace cluster next to Kempegowda International Airport.

02 — Our Approach

Specialist high-side plumbing + clean-room-grade interiors.

The civil shell was straightforward RCC. The complexity was in the high-side plumbing (industrial-grade lines for component cleaning), the interior finishes that had to meet aerospace cleanliness specifications, and the external development that integrated with KIADB's aerospace cluster road network.

  • Civil: RCC structure with epoxy industrial floors
  • High-side plumbing: High-pressure stainless-steel lines for component cleaning
  • Interiors: Clean-room-adjacent finishes — anti-static, easy-clean surfaces
  • External: Integration with KIADB aerospace cluster roads and utilities

03 — Result

Delivered on time. Operations qualified week one.

The facility was handed over within the 12-month commitment with full high-side plumbing commissioning. Rossell qualified the operational lines immediately and began aerospace manufacturing operations.

Aerospace or precision-manufacturing build?
