About SPC

Formerly known as SPG Constructions

Established in 2017, Suryapriya Constructions Pvt Ltd is a Bengaluru-based civil engineering firm with deep expertise in project management. Our construction services are widely appreciated and recommended by reputed clientele across the country — utilised in the past by many reputed industries.

From a single warehouse to a full turnkey campus, every project is delivered with disciplined planning, advanced equipment and an unwavering commitment to safety.

Sustainability

Building responsibly.

Construction has a footprint. We work to minimise ours — site by site, project by project.

Construction waste management

Segregated disposal, debris recycling at source, certified vendors for hazardous material.

Water conservation

On-site STPs, treated water for curing & landscaping, rainwater harvesting in delivered projects.

Energy-efficient builds

PEB systems, high-SRI roofs, façade insulation — aligned with IGBC green building principles.

Workforce welfare

On-site sanitation, drinking water, dignified labour quarters & safety equipment as standard.
